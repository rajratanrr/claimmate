# ClaimMate customer guide

An easy guide to keeping receipts, finding support and preparing your next step.

**Guided edition | October 2026**

## Start with one purchase

ClaimMate keeps your purchase details, receipts, policies and support requests together. A **purchase** is something you bought. A **case** is a saved support request about that purchase.

1. **Add your receipt.** Upload a clear photo or document.
2. **Check the details.** Correct the suggested information and confirm it.
3. **Add the policy.** Save the seller's return policy or the product warranty, if you have it.
4. **Describe the problem.** Use Assistant to prepare a support draft.
5. **Review and contact support.** Copy the message or download it, then send it yourself using the company's official support route.

> **You stay in control.** ClaimMate prepares the paperwork. It does not send emails, make calls, submit claims or approve refunds or warranty coverage.

## What to have ready

- Your receipt or invoice, including readable text.
- The product name and any order reference or serial number you have.
- A return policy or warranty document, if available.
- A short description of the problem and what you have already tried.

# 1. Open your workspace

## Create an account

1. Open the ClaimMate website link shared with you. For a local demonstration on the computer running the app, open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Enter your **Email** and a **Password** of at least 10 characters.
3. Select **Create my workspace**.
4. Next time, choose **Already have an account? Sign in**, enter the same details and select **Sign in**.

Use a unique password and keep it safe. This version has no email verification or password-reset feature. If you forget your password, contact the person running your ClaimMate site for help; do not expect an automated reset email.

> **Use the same website each time.** Your account belongs to that ClaimMate server. The local demo and a separately hosted website have separate accounts and saved purchases.

## Get to know the menu

- **Overview:** your purchase shelf, recent cases and recorded dates.
- **Purchase vault:** add, find and review your purchases. Search by product or merchant; use **Needs review** to find unfinished records.
- **Assistant:** describe a problem and prepare a draft.
- **Cases:** reopen saved requests and track progress.
- **Documents:** find saved files, view their text and download originals.
- **Alexa+ demo:** try the optional demonstration described on page 7.
- **Settings & help:** sample purchases, voice preferences, data export and account controls.

On a small screen, use the menu button to open navigation. To log out, select the **Sign out** icon beside your email.

## Try it without your own documents

Choose **Explore sample purchases** on an empty Overview, or **Settings & help > Add sample purchases**. Samples are fictional and marked as samples. Use them to practice the complete flow.

**Reset samples** resets those fictional purchases and their cases. Your personal purchases are preserved.

# 2. Upload and check a receipt

## Add a new purchase

1. Select **Add a purchase**.
2. Drop a receipt into **Drop a receipt photo or choose a file**, or select a file from your device.
3. Optionally enter the product name to make the record easier to identify.
4. Select **Read receipt & review** and wait for processing to finish.

No receipt yet? Enter a product name and choose **Continue to purchase**. You can fill in the details and upload a receipt later.

## What fills automatically?

ClaimMate reads printed text and suggests fields it can identify: product, merchant, purchase date, amount, currency, order or invoice reference, and serial number. It saves these as information for you to review.

Read the **Receipt review notes**. Check every field against the original, including the delivery date, category and currency. Correct errors, enter known missing values and leave unknown optional details empty. Select **Confirm reviewed details** to save your corrections and enable **Get help with this purchase**.

> **Automatic reading can make mistakes.** A date such as 03/04/2026 may be ambiguous. A photograph of a laptop without a printed receipt cannot reveal its price or purchase date. All filled fields still need your review.

## Add a receipt to an existing purchase

Open the purchase in **Purchase vault**. Under **Document type**, choose **Receipt - read & fill purchase details**, then select or drop the file. Upload starts immediately. Review the new suggestions and confirm the details again.

Choosing **Supporting evidence** stores a file but does not use it to fill purchase fields.

## Help the reader get it right

- Use a sharp, straight photo with good light and all receipt edges visible.
- Use **PNG, JPEG, PDF or TXT**, up to **10 MB per file**. PDFs can have up to **30 pages**. Convert HEIC photos to JPEG or PNG first.
- Image reading currently supports English. A scanned PDF may need separate page images or typed text.
- If reading fails, use **Retry extraction**, or expand **View extracted text / enter transcription**, enter corrected text and select **Save transcription**. Recheck purchase fields afterward; you can edit them directly.

# 3. Keep the policy and evidence

## Add the return or warranty policy

1. Open your purchase in **Purchase vault**.
2. Set **Document type** to **Return / warranty policy**.
3. Upload the policy file. Alternatively, expand **Or paste a policy**, paste the exact text and select **Save policy text**.
4. Read **What your policy actually says**. Open **View source** to inspect the supporting document passage.

Use the policy that applies to your purchase. Keep the seller's name, product, dates, exclusions and any special conditions in mind. If you do not have the policy, ask the seller for it; ClaimMate does not fetch it automatically.

## Add other supporting documents

Choose **Supporting evidence** before uploading relevant photos or documents, such as a photo of visible damage or a saved support reply. Supported file types are the same as for receipts; videos and email files are not supported directly.

Use **Download original** to retrieve a stored file. Uploading it to ClaimMate does not attach it to a message you send elsewhere.

## Optional: record a date from the policy

If the rule is clear, expand **Confirm a deadline from a passage**:

1. Choose the **Supporting passage** and **Policy type**: return or warranty.
2. Choose what starts the period under **Trigger**, such as purchase or delivery.
3. Enter the matching **Trigger date**. Check it again if you change the trigger; a purchase date may already be filled in.
4. Enter the **Period** and **Units**, or the explicit expiry date stated in the document.
5. Check whether the trigger date counts as day one. Confirm that you reviewed the passage and select **Save confirmed date**.

These dates appear in the app. This version does not send deadline emails or push notifications.

> **A recorded date is not an approval.** The company still needs to assess the problem and policy conditions. Leave the date unrecorded if the wording is unclear.

Replacing policy text or deleting a policy can remove linked recorded dates and affect old draft citations. Changing a purchase or delivery date, including through a newly read receipt, also clears deadlines tied to that date. Review and confirm those dates again. Use **Remove recorded date** to remove a date you entered incorrectly.

# 4. Ask for help and find support

## Describe what happened

1. Confirm your purchase details, then select **Get help with this purchase**, or open **Assistant** and choose the correct purchase.
2. Type a short message under **Tell me what happened...**, then select **Send**.
3. Read the guided response and any policy passages it shows.
4. Check **Issue for the support request**. Enter the problem you want included, then select **Prepare support draft**.

For example: "My headphones stopped charging yesterday. I tried a different cable, but the charging light still does not turn on."

Describe only what you know. Useful details include when the issue began, what happens, what you tried and any visible damage. If several purchases have similar names, choose the correct one before making the draft.

**Send** in Assistant saves a message to your ClaimMate conversation. It does not send anything to the seller. This is a guided assistant using saved details and documents, not a live AI chatbot.

## Find the company's support route

Look for **Where to ask for help** on a purchase or case page. ClaimMate matches your saved product and merchant names to its support directory.

1. Check the suggested company.
2. If needed, use **Confirm the company** to choose the correct brand.
3. Select **Open official contact page**.
4. Follow the company's instructions to find the available phone, chat, form or email option for your product.

> **Check the company and country.** The current directory covers selected brands in India: Dell, HP, Samsung, Sony, Apple, boAt, JBL and Lenovo. It is not a live search of every company. Use the official page to check current contact details and hours.

Not every company provides a direct email address or phone number. If there is no match, use the seller's receipt or official website. ClaimMate does not invent a contact address.

For a return or order problem, start with the seller named on your receipt. For a product fault, the manufacturer's support page may also help. The seller and manufacturer can be different companies.

# 5. Review, send and follow up

## Finish your case

Your prepared draft opens as a case. A case keeps the request, purchase evidence, notes and progress together.

1. Read the **Editable message**. Correct names, dates, references, amounts and descriptions. Replace any missing-information placeholders before using it.
2. Add useful context under **Issue details & notes**.
3. Review the **Evidence checklist** and available **Original attachments**.
4. Choose the appropriate **Status** and select **Save changes** or **Save case** before leaving.

Checklist ticks mean you marked an item ready. They do not attach files to an outgoing message or prove that a company received them.

## Choose a download or copy option

- **Copy message:** copies the current draft so you can paste it into an email or support form. Save the case separately to preserve edits.
- **TXT:** saves your current case edits and downloads the message as a text file.
- **PDF packet:** saves your current case edits and downloads a formatted packet with the request, saved details and supporting passages.
- **Original attachments:** downloads the original receipt and other saved files individually. These are separate from the PDF packet.

## Contact the company yourself

Open the official support route. Paste your reviewed message or follow the company's form instructions. Add the receipt, relevant evidence and PDF only where requested. Review the recipient and attachments before sending.

After contacting support, return to the case, record the company's reference number and response in **Issue details & notes**, select **user marked sent**, and save. Responses are not imported automatically.

## Understand the status choices

- **draft:** you are still writing the request.
- **needs evidence:** you need more documents or information.
- **ready for review:** the request is ready for your final check.
- **user marked sent:** you have contacted the company yourself.
- **resolved:** you consider the case finished.

To continue later, sign in to the same ClaimMate site, open **Cases** and select the case. Your saved notes and draft remain available. Use **Resume saved case** in a linked Assistant conversation when shown.

# 6. Voice and the Alexa+ demo

## Speak instead of typing

In **Assistant**, select the microphone button, **Start voice input**. Allow microphone access if your browser asks. Speak, select **Stop listening**, correct the transcript and then choose **Send**.

Use **Cancel** to discard the current voice capture. If speech input is unavailable or permission is denied, type your message instead. You can enable **Read responses aloud** and use **Stop audio** to stop playback.

Browser speech services may process audio remotely. Receipt text reading is separate and runs on the ClaimMate server.

## What Alexa+ means in this version

ClaimMate includes an **Alexa+ demo** for showing how a conversational assistant could use its tools to retrieve purchase evidence and prepare a support request.

The demonstration makes real calls to the ClaimMate server and saves real cases in your account. It uses a small client inside this website. It is **not connected to Amazon Alexa+**, an Echo device or an Amazon account. No language model is connected to this demo.

MCP is the connection format used by these tools. You do not need to understand it or create a developer token to use ClaimMate normally.

## Try the optional demonstration

Use a fictional sample purchase if you are showing the app to other people.

1. Confirm the purchase details first, then open **Alexa+ demo**.
2. Select **Connect to ClaimMate MCP**.
3. Choose the **Purchase** and select **Read documents & assess recorded periods**.
4. Enter the **Reported problem** and select **Prepare draft through MCP**.
5. Choose **Review & download packet** to open the saved case. **Resume same case** retrieves that case again.

The **Live protocol trace** shows actual server responses. It may display purchase and case information, so use fictional data for public demonstrations.

> **An accurate way to describe it:** "ClaimMate is an aftercare assistant with working purchase and support tools. This screen demonstrates a possible Alexa+ experience. Amazon account linking is not connected yet."

You can use all normal purchase and case features without opening the Alexa+ demo.

# 7. Your saved information

## Where your information goes

ClaimMate saves purchases, document text, cases and conversations on the server running your ClaimMate site. Original files are stored separately on that server. Your browser holds your sign-in session; it is not the main database.

With the same account, website and persistent server storage, saved information remains available after a refresh or server restart. A newly hosted site starts with its own database; it does not automatically copy records from a local demo.

Receipt text reading runs on that server. This guided edition does not send your receipt to an AI or external OCR service. The person operating the server manages its storage and backups. Only upload information you intend to keep with that service.

## Keep a copy of your records

1. Open **Settings & help**.
2. Select **Export records & extracted text** to download a JSON data file containing your saved records and extracted text.
3. Download original files separately from **Documents > Original**, or from **Original attachments** in a case.
4. Use **PDF packet** for a case you want to read or share easily.

The JSON export is for keeping a copy of your data; this version has no automatic import or restore button. It does not include the original image/PDF files.

## Remove information you no longer need

- **One document:** use its **Delete** control, or the trash icon in **Documents**. Removing a policy can also remove its source passages and linked dates.
- **One purchase:** open it and select **Delete purchase and related data**. This also removes its documents and related cases.
- **Your account:** open **Settings & help > Delete account and data**, then type `DELETE` when asked. This removes the account's purchases, documents, cases and conversations from the app.

> **Deletion is permanent in the app.** Export what you need first. Previously downloaded files and information already sent to a company are separate copies. Ask the site operator about any server backup copies.

Sign out when you finish on a shared device. Keep your password private. The **Developer connection: MCP** controls are optional; ordinary customers do not need to copy or share a token.

# 8. Quick answers and an example

## Something is not working

**The receipt fields are blank or incorrect.** Check that you uploaded a receipt with readable text and selected the Receipt document type. Try a clearer photo, correct the fields manually, or save a transcription. The app cannot fill information missing from the receipt.

**The upload fails.** Use PNG, JPEG, PDF or TXT, stay within 10 MB and keep PDFs within 30 pages. For a scanned PDF, try uploading each page as an image. Read the on-screen error before retrying.

**Get help or Prepare support draft will not work.** Confirm the purchase details, choose the correct purchase and enter a meaningful issue of at least five characters. If purchase selection cleared the issue, enter it again.

**There is no support contact.** Choose the brand manually if it appears in the directory. Otherwise use the seller's receipt or official website. The directory covers selected brands in India, not every company.

**My saved purchases are missing.** Check that you are on the same website and signed in with the same email. A local demo and a hosted site do not share data. If records still disappear, contact the site operator to check storage and backups.

**My edits disappeared.** Purchase changes need **Confirm reviewed details**. Case edits need **Save changes** or **Save case**. Copying a draft does not save it. Downloads save the current case edits first.

**Login fails or I forgot my password.** Check the email, password and website address. If you see too many attempts, wait a minute. This version has no automatic password reset; contact the site operator.

**The PDF does not contain my original receipt.** The PDF packet summarizes the request and evidence. Download the original receipt separately and attach it yourself when required.

## Example: headphones stop charging

1. Add the headphone receipt and check its product, seller, date and amount.
2. Confirm the details; add the warranty document if you have it.
3. Open **Assistant**, select the headphones and describe what happened.
4. Prepare the draft, correct it and save the case.
5. Open the official support page and contact the company with your reviewed message and requested attachments.
6. Save the response/reference number in your case and update its status.

**Remember the flow:** upload, review, prepare, contact, follow up.
